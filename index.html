WolfLLM: Training and Evaluating Large Language Models for Werewolf Social Deduction
AAAI 2027 Under Review审稿中
Yu Fang*, Junliang Ye*†
A real-game Werewolf corpus built from multi-speaker game videos, with player-perspective information reconstruction and a benchmark for identity inference and strategic speech generation. A 0.6B model fine-tuned on it raises identity-inference accuracy from 1.08% to 56.18%, the best among all evaluated models including Kimi-k2.6 (1.1T), DeepSeek-v4 and GLM-5.2. 将真实多人狼人杀对局视频转化为结构化语料，重建玩家视角信息状态，并构建身份推理与策略发言生成两项 Benchmark。 基于该数据微调的 0.6B 模型将身份推理准确率从 1.08% 提升至 56.18%， 超过 Kimi-k2.6（1.1T）、DeepSeek-v4、GLM-5.2 等全部对比模型。
